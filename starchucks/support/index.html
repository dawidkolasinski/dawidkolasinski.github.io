Support

Last updated: November 17, 2026

For help with Starchucks, email sunchucks.support@gmail.com. This is our existing support inbox for the app family; include Starchucks in your message.

Report a problem

Include the app version, device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce the problem. If helpful, attach a screenshot and say whether the issue concerns a purchase. Send only the information needed for your request; you do not need to share your exact observation location.

Sky position and AR alignment

Check the selected place, date, time and time zone. An object may be below the horizon, outside the camera direction or obscured by daylight or local conditions. Check location, camera and motion permissions in iOS Settings, and use the app’s calibration controls when needed. The star catalog and calculations are local; online maps and search need a network connection.

Premium and subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen to restore an eligible App Store purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account: Apple’s subscription instructions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Optional rewarded ads, where available, grant 30 minutes of temporary Premium only after the reward is earned. Ad availability can depend on connectivity, privacy choices and the advertising service.

Privacy choices

The analytics preference in Starchucks Settings controls Analytics and Performance Monitoring. Advertising privacy options appear when required. iOS Settings controls system permissions and tracking. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

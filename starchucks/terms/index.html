Where advertising is available, completing an optional rewarded ad grants 30 minutes of Premium access. This does not start or renew a paid subscription. Ads may be unavailable; closing an ad before earning the reward does not grant access.

Astronomical planning

Starchucks calculates sky positions and planning windows for the selected observation place, date and time. These calculations do not guarantee that an object will be visible. Daylight, terrain, buildings, clouds, light pollution and your equipment can affect observations. The app does not currently provide a weather forecast.

AR alignment depends on camera and motion-sensor quality, location and calibration. Use it as an observing aid; it is not a navigation or surveying instrument. Observe from a safe place and never point an optical instrument at the Sun without appropriate protection.

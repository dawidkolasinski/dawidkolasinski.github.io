Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Starchucks is an iOS app provided by Dawid Kolasiński, who is responsible for the data practices described here. For privacy requests, contact sunchucks.support@gmail.com and mention Starchucks.

Observation places and maps

With your permission, Starchucks uses your location to calculate the sky for your observation place. You can select a place manually. Saved places, observation preferences and other app settings are stored on your device. The star catalog and astronomical calculations are available locally. Apple may process map, place-search and geocoding requests when you use those services.

Camera, motion and notifications

The camera and motion sensors align stars and constellations with the augmented reality view. Camera frames are processed for this view and are not automatically uploaded to a server operated by us. iOS permissions control camera, location, motion and notification access for the features you choose.

Analytics and diagnostics

Starchucks uses Firebase Analytics to understand app use, Firebase Performance Monitoring to diagnose performance, and Firebase Crashlytics to investigate crashes. Google may process app-use events, app-instance or installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app-start and network-performance measurements, CPU and memory usage, crash reports and stack traces. Analytics derives general location from masked IP addresses. Performance Monitoring can also use IP addresses to group performance data by region. Analytics may measure App Store purchase and subscription events, including product identifiers, names and prices.

You can disable Analytics and Performance Monitoring through the analytics preference in Starchucks Settings. This preference does not control Crashlytics collection or advertising choices. Development and simulator builds suppress Firebase collection.

Advertising and your choices

When advertising is enabled, Starchucks uses Google AdMob for a Home banner and optional rewarded ads. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad impressions and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information for advertising and measurement. Access to the advertising identifier depends on your iOS tracking permission.

Google User Messaging Platform presents applicable privacy messages. Where required, advertising privacy options in Starchucks Settings let you revisit your choices. Manage tracking permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads may be limited or unavailable depending on your choices and service availability.

Purchases and support

Apple handles purchases, subscriptions and payment information through the App Store. Starchucks receives transaction and entitlement information to unlock or restore Premium; we do not receive your card details. If you earn temporary Premium through a rewarded ad, its expiry is stored on your device. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you send.

Providers, retention and rights

Providers include Google Firebase and AdMob and Apple. They may process data in other countries under their own policies and safeguards. Starchucks does not require a user account.

Local app data remains until you remove it or uninstall the app, subject to your device backups. Provider data follows the relevant retention policies; see Firebase privacy information. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle your request. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of data we can identify and control, or to exercise applicable objection, restriction or portability rights. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

This website and updates

This website uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate the site. This policy may be updated when Starchucks data practices change. The date above identifies the current published version.

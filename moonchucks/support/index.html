Support

Last updated: October 26, 2026

For help with Moonchucks, email sunchucks.support@gmail.com. This is the existing support inbox; include Moonchucks in your message so we can identify the app.

Useful details

Include the app version, device model, iOS version and steps that reproduce the issue. If helpful, attach a screenshot and mention whether the issue concerns a purchase. Only include information needed for your request.

Finding the Moon

Check the selected place, date and time zone. The Moon may be below the horizon or outside the camera direction. AR alignment uses your phone's camera, location and motion sensors; review the relevant permissions in iOS Settings.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel App Store subscriptions in your Apple Account. Restore an eligible purchase using Restore Purchases on the Moonchucks Premium screen.

Moonchucks includes advertising. Where offered, completing an optional rewarded ad grants 30 minutes of Premium access. Rewarded access does not start or renew a subscription. Ads may not always be available.

Planning information

Lunar positions and events are calculated for the selected place, date and time. Actual visibility also depends on weather, terrain and the view of the horizon. NOAA aurora information is modeled forecast data and does not guarantee visible aurora. NASA Night lights is artificial-light imagery from 2016, not a live sky-brightness measurement or a Bortle classification.

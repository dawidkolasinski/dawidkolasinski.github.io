Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Moonchucks is an iOS app provided by Dawid Kolasiński. For privacy requests, contact sunchucks.support@gmail.com and mention Moonchucks.

Location and maps

With iOS permission, Moonchucks can use your current location for lunar planning. You can also select a place manually. Saved places and preferences are stored on your device. Coordinates needed for weather forecasts are sent to MET Norway. Apple services may process map and place-search requests.

Camera, motion and notifications

The camera and motion sensors support the augmented reality view. Camera frames are processed for that view and are not automatically uploaded to a server operated by us. System permissions control access to the camera, location, photos and notifications used by the features you choose.

Analytics and diagnostics

Moonchucks uses Firebase Analytics, Performance Monitoring and Crashlytics. Google may process app-use events, installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app-start and network-performance metrics, crash reports and stack traces. Performance Monitoring may use IP addresses to estimate a general region. Firebase Remote Config supplies app configuration. These services help us understand use, configure the app and diagnose problems.

Online sky information

Moon appearance and nighttime-light imagery are requested from NASA services. Aurora information is requested from NOAA. Online providers and their hosting services may receive IP addresses and request information when these features are used. The selected map region determines the imagery requested.

Purchases and support

If you earn temporary Premium access by watching an optional rewarded ad, Moonchucks stores its expiry time on your device so access can continue until it expires, including after reopening the app.

Apple handles subscription purchases and payment details through the App Store. Moonchucks receives the transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock or restore Premium. We do not receive your card details. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include.

Advertising and privacy choices

Moonchucks uses Google AdMob for a Home banner and optional rewarded ads. Google may process device identifiers, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic and performance data for advertising and measurement. Access to the advertising identifier is subject to iOS tracking permission.

Google User Messaging Platform manages applicable consent messages. When required, advertising privacy options in the app's Settings let you revisit your choices. You can manage iOS tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Your choices can affect the ads available.

Providers

Providers include Google Firebase and AdMob, Apple, MET Norway, NASA and NOAA. They may process information in different countries under their own policies. Moonchucks does not require a user account.

Retention and your choices

Saved places and app preferences remain on your device until removed or the app is uninstalled. Provider data is subject to the providers' retention policies. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request. Manage system permissions in iOS Settings and subscriptions in your Apple Account. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information we can identify and control.

This website and changes

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate the site. The current Moonchucks policy is published on this page and may be updated when the app's data practices change.

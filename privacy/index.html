Privacy Policy

Effective September 24, 2026

Sunchucks is an iOS app provided by Dawid Kolasiński. For privacy requests, contact sunchucks.support@gmail.com.

Data and purposes

  • Location and weather. With iOS permission, the app uses your current or selected location for maps, solar calculations and forecasts. Coordinates needed for forecasts go to MET Norway. Maps and place search may use Apple services. Saved places and preferences are stored on your device.
  • Camera and reminders. Camera frames are used for the augmented reality view and are not automatically uploaded to a server run by us. iOS handles reminders under the permission you give.
  • Analytics and performance. Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring are off on a fresh installation. You can enable and disable them using the “Share usage and performance data” switch in app Settings. When enabled, they process app-use events, app-state properties, device and operating-system data, app-start metrics and network-request metrics. Firebase Performance can use IP addresses to estimate a general region. Our custom Analytics events do not contain coordinates, place names, email addresses or user content. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google UMP consent signals may further restrict Analytics collection. Advertising choices are available separately.
  • Crash reports. Firebase Crashlytics automatically receives crash reports, stack traces, relevant app state and device/OS details to help fix problems. We do not attach custom user IDs or coordinates.
  • Advertising. Home may show a banner and an optional rewarded ad that grants 30 minutes of Premium access. Google AdMob and its partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers available under your permissions, ad interactions and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. The app does not pass selected coordinates to AdMob. Google User Messaging Platform collects advertising choices where required. Denying iOS tracking permission does not block ads, but restricts use of the advertising identifier and cross-app tracking. Declining ad personalization may still allow non-personalized or limited ads, depending on your region and consent choices. The app does not request an ad when Google UMP does not permit it, and an eligible request may still return no ad.
  • Purchases. Apple processes subscriptions through the App Store and StoreKit. The app receives the transaction and entitlement status required to unlock or restore Premium; we do not receive card details.

Providers and sharing

Data goes to providers only as needed for the features above: Google (AdMob, Firebase and UMP), Apple and MET Norway. They may process data in different countries under their own policies. Data transfers in the ad system may count as a “sale” or “sharing” under some US state laws; eligible users can opt out through the app's privacy choices. Sunchucks does not require an account with us or run a user-account server.

Retention and choices

Settings and saved places remain on your device until removed or until the app is uninstalled. Data processed by Google, Apple and MET Norway is also subject to those providers' retention practices, which we do not fully control. Manage location, camera, notification and tracking permissions in iOS Settings. You can turn off analytics and performance collection in Sunchucks Settings at any time; this does not delete previously collected data. Where required for your region, use “Privacy choices” in Sunchucks Settings to revisit ad consent. Manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings. Contact us to request access, correction, objection or deletion; we will respond for data we can identify and control.

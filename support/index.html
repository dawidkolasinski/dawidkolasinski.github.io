Support

Need help with Sunchucks? Contact us at sunchucks.support@gmail.com.

When Contacting Support

Include these details if they are relevant:

  • App name and version
  • Device model
  • iOS version
  • Steps that reproduce the issue
  • Screenshots or screen recordings, if useful
  • Whether the issue involves an App Store purchase or subscription

Subscriptions

App Store subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID subscription settings. You can cancel, upgrade, downgrade, or restore purchases using Apple's subscription management tools.
